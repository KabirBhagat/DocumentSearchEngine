Last weekend, I visited a new restaurant with my friends.
The restaurant was located near the city center.
It had a comfortable atmosphere and friendly staff.

We arrived at around 8:00 PM.
The restaurant was crowded because many people
were enjoying their dinner.

We ordered pizza, pasta, and fresh fruit juice.
The food was delicious, but the service was
a little slow. My favorite dish was the cheese pizza.

The restaurant had a large menu with different
types of food. Some people preferred vegetarian
dishes, while others ordered non-vegetarian meals.

The staff were friendly and helped us choose
different dishes. We also ordered a chocolate cake
for dessert.

The total bill was ₹1,250.
The prices were reasonable considering the quality
of the food and the location of the restaurant.

After dinner, we spent some time talking about
our work, travel plans, and daily experiences.
Everyone enjoyed the evening.

A good restaurant should provide quality food,
friendly service, and a comfortable place
where people can spend time together.

We enjoyed our dinner and decided to visit
the restaurant again.
