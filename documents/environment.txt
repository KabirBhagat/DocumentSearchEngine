Environmental protection is important for future generations.
People should take care of the environment and protect
the natural resources available to them.

Air pollution is a major problem in many cities.
Vehicles and factories release harmful gases into
the atmosphere. These gases can affect the health
of people and animals.

Water pollution is another important environmental issue.
Many factories release waste into rivers and lakes.
People also throw plastic bags and other waste
into water bodies.

Planting trees can help improve air quality.
Trees provide fresh air and shelter for birds
and other animals. They also make cities
more beautiful and comfortable.

People can make a difference through small changes
in their daily lives. Using public transportation,
saving water, and reducing plastic waste are
some useful steps.

Many people participate in environmental campaigns.
Different organizations organize events to encourage
people to protect nature.

Environmental problems affect different places
in different ways. Some cities experience severe
air pollution, while other places face water shortages.

Protecting the environment requires cooperation.
People, businesses, and governments must work together.
A clean environment is important for a healthy life.
